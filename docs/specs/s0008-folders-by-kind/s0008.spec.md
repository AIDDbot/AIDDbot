# s0008 - Group every brain document in a kind folder

## Problem

### Goal

After [`s0007`](../s0007-separate-brain-and-wiki/s0007.spec.md), the living pages are in one folder for each kind. Three exceptions remain: the specs in `docs/specs/`, the `notepad` in `docs/`, and `docs/PLAN.md`, which mixes product and process. The folders of `docs/` must show the mental map of the brain. This change applies the agreed proposals F1 to F7 of the [documentation catalog](../../process/documentation-catalog.md#folders-that-mirror-the-kinds).

### User stories

- `US1` - As a person or an agent who works on AIDDbot, I want every brain document under `docs/` in the folder of its kind, so that the folders show the map of the brain.

## Solution

1. **Move the documents** with `git mv`:

   | From | To | Proposal |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | `docs/specs/` | `docs/product/specs/` | F1, F4 |
   | `docs/NOTEPAD.txt` | `docs/process/NOTEPAD.txt` | F2 |

   The root documents (`README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`) stay at the root (F5).
2. **Split `docs/PLAN.md`** (F3, F7), then delete it:
   - The building order, the tasks of each milestone, and the future ideas, in a Later section, go into `ROADMAP.md`.
   - The dogfooding rules, how to use the plan, and the findings go to a new page, `docs/process/dogfooding.md`.
3. **Documentation catalog.** A `spec` and the change documents in its folder have the fixed Kind `PRODUCT` (F1, F4). Remove the sentence that lets change documents stay outside the kind folders. Add a decision for the change, and remove the Pending section "Folders that mirror the kinds".
4. **Scaffolding.** Update the layout and "When each folder appears".
5. **Explorer.** Read the specs from `docs/product/specs/`. Remove the `docs/PLAN.md` group and its `PLAN.md` alias, and read `dogfooding.md` with the process pages. Update the content scope in `explorer-development.md`. The seven tables of the brain contract keep their headings and columns.
6. **Links and other documents.** Update every relative link, `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, the wiki index, and `CHANGELOG.md`. A closed spec changes only in its links.

## Verification

- [ ] `US1R1` - The repository shall keep the specs in `docs/product/specs/` and the `notepad` in `docs/process/`, and it shall have no `docs/PLAN.md`: its parts shall be in `ROADMAP.md` and `docs/process/dogfooding.md`.
- [ ] `US1R2` - The documentation catalog, the scaffolding page, and the wiki index shall state F1 to F7 with no contradiction, and the catalog shall have no Pending section "Folders that mirror the kinds".
- [ ] `US1R3` - When a person runs the link check, `bun test`, `bunx tsc --noEmit`, and the explorer build, each one shall pass with no broken relative link or anchor.
